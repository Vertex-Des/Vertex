About Vertex

We started Vertex because Desmos already sits on every SAT test-taker's screen — most students just never learn to actually use it.

Our mission

Every Digital SAT comes with a built-in Desmos graphing calculator, but most prep resources teach math the same way they did before the calculator existed. Vertex teaches students to reach for graphing, regression, and Desmos's statistics tools first — turning a five-minute algebra problem into a thirty-second graph read. We're starting small: a friend group chat and a school, with plans to grow from there.
